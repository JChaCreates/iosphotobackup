Privacy Policy

Photo Import & Share

Effective 10 October 2026

Photo Import & Share collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. The app has no networking of any kind other than Apple's purchase system.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app records stays on your device or in the folder you chose. Specifically:

  • Import history — a local database remembers which files this app has imported and where, so they still show as Already Imported even if renamed. It holds file names, sizes, dates and the folder each one went to. It lives in the app's own storage and is deliberately excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups. You can clear it at any time in Settings.
  • Fingerprints — when Full Hash duplicate detection is on, or when you run Scan Apple Photos in Settings, the app keeps a short code computed from each file's contents so the next check is faster. A fingerprint is not a copy of the photo and cannot be turned back into one. Once you've scanned your library, photos added since are fingerprinted the next time you import. Clear them in Settings, which also starts the scan over.
  • An index of your Apple Photos library — when you import into Apple Photos, the app keeps a list of the file names and capture times already in your library, so it can tell what's new. This is read from your library with your permission, stored only on the device, and never leaves it.
  • Cached thumbnails are kept so browsing a folder a second time is fast. The app trims them automatically.
  • Preferences — your import options and appearance — are stored in the app's own settings. Which card or folder you chose is remembered only while the app is open; closing the app forgets it, and with it the app's permission to read that folder.
  • The free trial's start date is kept in your device's keychain, so reinstalling the app doesn't restart the trial. It is only a date, and it never leaves your device.
  • Today's import count — after the trial, the app counts imports made today so it can apply the daily limit. The count resets each day and is stored in the app's own settings.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it except the trial start date in the keychain.

Your photos and your memory card

The app reads a memory card either when you plug a card reader in (iOS tells the app a camera card has arrived, and asks you to allow access the first time) or from a folder you explicitly choose through the iOS file picker. It never writes to the card, changes anything on it, or deletes from it.

Photos you import are copied into Apple Photos or into the folder you selected, and nowhere else. The app has no access to your camera, your location, your contacts, or anything else on the device.

Apple Photos access

Importing into Apple Photos needs permission to add to your library. Telling which items are already there needs permission to read it. Both are asked for by iOS the first time you need them, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos. With "Add Photos Only" or limited access the app still imports, but treats everything on the card as new.

Sharing

Sharing uses Apple's standard share sheet. What happens after you pick a destination there is governed by that app or service, not by this one. This app sends nothing anywhere on its own.

Purchases

The unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Payment details are never seen by, or shared with, this app or its developer. The app asks Apple whether a purchase exists; that is the whole of the exchange.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. No usage statistics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics libraries. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Since no personal data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Any change that affects what the app does with your data would arrive alongside an app update describing it.
