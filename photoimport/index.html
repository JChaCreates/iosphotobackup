Photo Import & Share

Plug in a memory card, see what's new, and bring it into Apple Photos or a folder in a tap.

Support Privacy

For iPhone and iPad. Plug a card reader into your device and the card appears by itself. Every photo and video on it is labelled New or Already Imported, so you bring in only what you don't have yet.

What it does

  • Import — into Apple Photos or any folder in Files. Select New, select all, or pick by hand. A RAW + JPEG pair goes into Photos as one photo with the RAW kept attached, the way Apple's own importer does it.
  • Duplicate detection — File Name (Quick) matches by name and capture time in seconds; Full Hash (Slow) compares the contents of every file so a renamed photo is still caught; or turn it off and choose yourself.
  • Browse and share — look through the card or any folder you imported to: zoom, swipe, see when and how each shot was taken, and share. Share the items you just imported straight from the Finished screen.

Made for cameras

  • Formats — RAW from Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, OM System, Panasonic, Pentax, Leica, Samsung, Hasselblad and Phase One, plus DNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, PNG, WebP and video (MOV, MP4). The File Formats screen in the app lets you switch any of them off.
  • Safe — nothing on the card is ever changed or deleted. Pull the card out mid-import and the app stops cleanly; plug it back in and carry on.
  • iPad — thumbnails and a large preview side by side, with keyboard shortcuts.
  • Keeps going — an optional setting lets a long import keep running while you use other apps (iOS 26 or later).

Nothing leaves your device. No account, no analytics, no uploads. See the privacy policy for what that means in detail.

Price

Free to download, with unlimited imports for 10 days. After that, 5 imports a day (a RAW + JPEG pair or a video counts as one). A single one-time purchase removes the limit, forever. Browsing and sharing are always free.

Requires

iOS or iPadOS 18.6 or later. A card reader or drive connected to your device — the same ones the Files app can see.
