Privacy Policy

PhotoBackup

Effective 2 September 2026

Home Support

PhotoBackup collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. The app has no networking of any kind other than Apple's purchase system.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app records stays on your device or on the drive you chose. Specifically:

  • Ratings and keywords are written to XMP sidecar files, saved next to the photos they describe — on your card or your drive, in the standard format Lightroom, Capture One and Bridge read.
  • A local database tracks which files have passed a backup check, and progress through an interrupted run. It lives in the app's own storage and is deliberately excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups.
  • Cached thumbnails are kept so browsing a folder a second time is fast. You can clear them at any time, and the app trims them automatically.
  • Preferences — your keyboard shortcuts, viewer options, and which folders a run was using — are stored in the app's own settings.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Your photos

The app can only read folders you explicitly choose through the iOS file picker. It is never given access to your Photos library, your camera, your location, your contacts, or anything else on the device.

Access to a folder you pick is held only while it's needed and released shortly after you move on. Your photos are copied from the card to the drive you selected and nowhere else.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Payment details are never seen by, or shared with, this app or its developer. The app asks Apple whether a purchase exists; that is the whole of the exchange.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. No usage statistics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics libraries. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Since no personal data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Any change that affects what the app does with your data would arrive alongside an app update describing it.
