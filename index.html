PhotoBackup

Offload a card to a drive from your iPhone or iPad — and know every file arrived intact.

Support Privacy

Copy a memory card to an external drive in the field, without a laptop. Then prove the copy is good before the card gets formatted.

What it does

  • Backup — copy a card to a drive, all of it or a range by filename or date, filtered to RAW, JPEG or everything. Interrupted runs pick up where they left off.
  • Verify — check every copied file against the original, by size or by SHA-256 hash. Files that don't match are listed, and can be re-copied.
  • Browse — review the shoot on the device: stars, pick and reject flags, keywords, written to XMP sidecars that Lightroom, Capture One and Bridge read.

Nothing leaves your device. No account, no analytics, no uploads. See the privacy policy for what that means in detail.

Requires

iOS or iPadOS 18.6 or later. An external drive or card reader connected to your device — the same ones the Files app can see.
